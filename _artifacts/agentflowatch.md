[AgentFloWatch](https://github.com/elrensmin/AgentFloWatch): an accountability system for autonomous agents. Monitor multi-agent (LangGraph) executions against a declared ideal path. Detect violations, measure waste/loops, and generate daily/weekly/monthly reports. Storage: PostgreSQL (policies) + ClickHouse (events).
