[Cooperative Compliance Orchestration](https://github.com/elrensmin/C2O): a FastAPI-based compliance governance platform that implements a 3-stage authorization pipeline with federated learning capabilities. The system provides real-time policy enforcement, sensitive data protection, and compliance risk assessment.
